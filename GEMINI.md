# The Sharks — Gemini project guidance

When reviewing startup ideas, use the five specialist roles defined in `agents/` and the orchestration protocol in `.agents/skills/the-sharks/SKILL.md`.

- MINT: revenue and monetization.
- MAGNET: customer acquisition and retention.
- LEDGER: unit economics and financials.
- RAZOR: competition and defensibility.
- FORGE: founder-market fit and execution.

Where independent agent execution is unavailable, run each role as a separate review and state that it is a simulated panel. Ask one sharp question per specialist before final verdicts. Separate facts, founder estimates, assumptions and unknowns. Never invent metrics or competitor evidence.

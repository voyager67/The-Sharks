# Example session: a focused startup reality check

> Fictional teaching example. It is not a market study, actual investment offer or financial recommendation.

**Founder:** I want to start TiffinBoss, a monthly tiffin subscription for students in PGs. Proposed price ₹3,000/month. Estimated cooking and delivery cost ₹2,400/month. I will acquire customers through college WhatsApp groups.

**THE SHARKS:** One question from each specialist:

- **MINT:** Have any students or parents paid, pre-ordered or explicitly committed at ₹3,000/month?
- **MAGNET:** Which specific groups or student communities can you reach, and what response do you expect from your first 50 targeted offers?
- **LEDGER:** Does ₹2,400 include labour, packaging, delivery, payment fees, refunds and food wastage?
- **RAZOR:** What do students currently use, and what would make them leave that option?
- **FORGE:** Who can fulfil the first 20 orders reliably, and what evidence do you have that you can manage food quality and delivery?

**After answers:** the panel produces evidence-linked IN/OUT verdicts, a Deal Meter, KILL SHOT and ranked Fix List. The answers and results—not improved pitch language—determine whether verdicts change.

# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or point of view.

## Our standards

Examples of constructive behavior include:
- Critiquing ideas, claims and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Being direct without being demeaning.
- Accepting good-faith correction and different perspectives.
- Protecting private, sensitive and confidential information.
- Giving actionable feedback and focusing on what helps the project improve.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, doxxing, threats, unwanted disclosure of private information or sustained bad-faith disruption.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary.

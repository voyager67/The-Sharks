# FAQ

## Are these five independent models?
Only when the host supports and is configured for real subagent execution. Otherwise The Sharks runs five separate role analyses in one conversation and must disclose that it is a simulated panel.

## Does a Deal Meter predict success?
No. It counts how many specialists currently say IN. It is a structured review output, not a probability, investment rating or guarantee.

## Does it use current competitor data?
RAZOR should research and cite current sources when browsing is available and current facts matter. Without research access, competitor claims are marked unverified.

## What if numbers are missing?
LEDGER asks for the most important missing input or presents formulas with unknown values. It must not invent costs, conversion, CAC, churn or revenue.

## Is this financial or investment advice?
No. It is a practice and decision-support framework, not financial, legal, tax or investment advice.
